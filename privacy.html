Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how wavelength.jonyen.com (the "Service") handles your information.

Information Collection and Use

The Service collects nothing. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking scripts. Nothing you do in the game is transmitted anywhere.

Local Storage

The game saves your in-progress game state (scores, current round, team names) in your browser's local storage so a refresh does not lose your game. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Cookies

The Service sets no cookies.

Server Logs

The Service is hosted as static files on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests, including IP addresses, as part of operating their infrastructure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Third-Party Assets

Two assets load from third parties: web fonts from Google Fonts and a confetti animation library from jsDelivr. Requesting those files exposes your IP address to those providers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
