Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how wavelength.jonyen.com (the "Service") handles your information.

Information Collection and Use

The Service keeps anonymous, cookieless, aggregate visit counts via GoatCounter; no personal data. There is no account system and no advertising, and nothing you do in the game (team names, scores, clues) is transmitted anywhere.

Visit Counts

Each page asks GoatCounter to count one visit. GoatCounter sees your IP address and browser with that request. It uses them to tell one visit from another for up to eight hours and to work out browser and country totals, and it stores neither: no IP address, no cookie, and no identifier for you, only totals such as visits per day and counts by referring site, browser, country, and screen size. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Local Storage

The game saves your in-progress game state (scores, current round, team names) in your browser's local storage so a refresh does not lose your game. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Cookies

The Service sets no cookies, and GoatCounter sets none either.

Server Logs

The Service is hosted as static files on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests, including IP addresses, as part of operating their infrastructure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Third-Party Assets

Three things load from third parties: web fonts from Google Fonts, a confetti animation library from jsDelivr, and the GoatCounter counting script (from gc.zgo.at, reporting to goatcounter.com). Requesting those files exposes your IP address to those providers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
