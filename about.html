About

This is a personal, self-hosted copy of a pass-and-play Wavelength game, put up for playing with friends and family in person. It is not a commercial product: no ads, no analytics, no accounts.

How It Works

Everything runs in your browser on one device. Gather around a phone, tablet, or a screen everyone can see, and pass it as turns change. There is no networked multiplayer — everyone plays from the same screen.

Credits

The original web implementation was written by mikeck1 and is available at mikeck1.github.io. Go play it there and say thanks. This copy removes the advertising and analytics and changes nothing else about the game.

Wavelength is a board game designed by Wolfgang Warsch, Alex Hague, and Justin Vickers, published by CMYK. This is an unofficial digital adaptation and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the publisher.
